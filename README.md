# animaltrackr
A primarily 3d-printed turret capable of tracking, taking pictures of, and (eventually) identifying passing wildlife. Also has a watergun feature for anti bird/cat poop feature.  
Features:
 - 360 degree yaw/20ish degree pitch movement of the water squirter/camera
 - Motion tracking - Day/night picture taking
 - Water gun for deterrence
 - LCD menu controlled by two buttons/joystick
 - Motion detection through PIR sensor
 - *Theoretically* self-sufficient given enough sun 
*Note about cart The BOM lists the regular non-discounted prices of the items
